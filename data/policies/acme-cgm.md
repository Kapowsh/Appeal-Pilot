# Acme Health Medical Policy AH-DME-019: Continuous Glucose Monitoring Systems

Fictional policy for a synthetic-data demo. Not a real payer's coverage policy.

## 1. Scope
This policy applies to therapeutic continuous glucose monitors (CGM) for members with diabetes mellitus.

## 2. Coverage Criteria
Continuous glucose monitor is considered medically necessary when ALL of the following criteria are met:

2.1 Diabetes mellitus treated with insulin by multiple daily injections or an insulin pump, or a documented history of problematic hypoglycemia (level 2 or level 3 events).
2.2 An in-person or telehealth visit with the prescribing practitioner within the past 6 months to evaluate glycemic control.
2.3 The member or caregiver has been trained and is able to use the device as prescribed.

## 3. Exclusions
3.1 Diabetes managed with diet or oral agents alone and no history of problematic hypoglycemia.

## 4. Documentation Requirements
4.1 Insulin regimen or hypoglycemia history, visit date, and documentation of device training.
