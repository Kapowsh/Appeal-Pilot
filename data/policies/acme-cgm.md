# Acme Health Medical Policy AH-DME-019: Continuous Glucose Monitoring Systems

Fictional policy for a synthetic-data demo. Adapted from CMS Local Coverage Determination L33822, Glucose Monitors (rev. 10/01/2024) (https://www.cms.gov/medicare-coverage-database/view/lcd.aspx?lcdid=33822). The criteria are paraphrased and simplified; this is not the current coverage policy of any real payer.

## 1. Scope
This policy applies to continuous glucose monitors (CGM) for members with diabetes mellitus.

## 2. Coverage Criteria
Continuous glucose monitor is considered medically necessary when ALL of the following criteria are met:

2.1 The member has diabetes mellitus.
2.2 The CGM is prescribed to improve glycemic control, and the member is insulin-treated or has a history of problematic hypoglycemia (recurrent level 2 events below 54 mg/dL despite more than one treatment adjustment, or one level 3 event requiring third-party assistance).
2.3 An in-person or telehealth visit with the treating practitioner within 6 months before the order to evaluate diabetes control.

## 3. Exclusions and Exceptions
3.1 Members treated with oral agents only and without problematic hypoglycemia.

## 4. Documentation Requirements
4.1 Diagnosis, insulin regimen or hypoglycemia history, and the visit date. A follow-up visit is required every 6 months.
