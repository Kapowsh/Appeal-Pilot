# Northwind Care Medical Policy NWC-DME-305: Continuous Glucose Monitors

Fictional policy for a synthetic-data demo. Adapted from CMS Local Coverage Determination L33822, Glucose Monitors (rev. 10/01/2024) (https://www.cms.gov/medicare-coverage-database/view/lcd.aspx?lcdid=33822). The criteria are paraphrased and simplified; this is not the current coverage policy of any real payer.

## 1. Scope
Northwind Care covers continuous glucose monitors (CGM) for members with diabetes.

## 2. Coverage Criteria
Continuous glucose monitor is considered medically necessary when ALL of the following criteria are met:

2.1 The member has diabetes mellitus.
2.2 The treating practitioner has concluded that the member or caregiver has sufficient training to use the prescribed CGM.
2.3 The CGM is prescribed in accordance with its FDA indications for use.
2.4 The CGM is prescribed to improve glycemic control, and the member is insulin-treated or has a history of problematic hypoglycemia (recurrent level 2 events below 54 mg/dL that persist despite more than one attempt to adjust treatment, or one level 3 event requiring third-party assistance).
2.5 Within 6 months before the order, the treating practitioner had an in-person or approved telehealth visit with the member to evaluate diabetes control.

## 3. Exclusions and Exceptions
3.1 Members treated with oral agents only and without problematic hypoglycemia (insulin does not exist in an oral form).

## 4. Documentation Requirements
4.1 Diagnosis, insulin regimen or hypoglycemia log, training documentation, and the visit date.
