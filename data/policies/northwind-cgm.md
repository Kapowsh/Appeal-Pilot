# Northwind Care Medical Policy NWC-DME-305: Continuous Glucose Monitors

Fictional policy for a synthetic-data demo. Not a real payer's coverage policy.

## 1. Scope
Northwind Care covers therapeutic continuous glucose monitors for members with diabetes.

## 2. Coverage Criteria
Continuous glucose monitor is considered medically necessary when ALL of the following criteria are met:

2.1 Diagnosis of diabetes mellitus.
2.2 Insulin therapy with at least one daily injection, or documented level 2 hypoglycemia events (glucose below 54 mg/dL).
2.3 Evaluation by the treating practitioner within 6 months before the order.

## 3. Exclusions
3.1 Non-therapeutic or adjunctive devices for members not using insulin and without hypoglycemia.

## 4. Documentation Requirements
4.1 Diagnosis, insulin regimen or hypoglycemia log, and the visit date.
