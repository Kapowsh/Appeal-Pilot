# Acme Health Medical Policy AH-ORTH-031: Arthroscopic Meniscectomy of the Knee

Fictional policy for a synthetic-data demo. Not a real payer's coverage policy.

## 1. Scope
This policy applies to arthroscopic partial meniscectomy for symptomatic meniscal tears in adult members.

## 2. Coverage Criteria
Knee arthroscopy is considered medically necessary when ALL of the following criteria are met:

2.1 Mechanical symptoms of the knee, such as locking, catching, or giving way.
2.2 MRI of the knee confirming a meniscal tear that corresponds to the symptoms.
2.3 Failure of at least 6 weeks of conservative therapy, including physical therapy, activity modification, NSAIDs, or intra-articular injection.
2.4 Absence of advanced osteoarthritis, defined as Kellgren-Lawrence grade 2 or lower on weight-bearing radiographs.

## 3. Exclusions
3.1 Arthroscopic debridement or lavage for osteoarthritis alone.
3.2 Degenerative tears without mechanical symptoms.

## 4. Documentation Requirements
4.1 Examination findings, MRI report, radiograph report with osteoarthritis grade, and dates of conservative therapy.
