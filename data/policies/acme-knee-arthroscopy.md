# Acme Health Medical Policy AH-ORTH-031: Arthroscopic Meniscectomy of the Knee

Fictional policy for a synthetic-data demo. Adapted from Aetna Clinical Policy Bulletin 0673, Knee Arthroscopy / Osteoarthritis of the Knee (https://www.aetna.com/cpb/medical/data/600_699/0673.html). The criteria are paraphrased and simplified; this is not the current coverage policy of any real payer.

## 1. Scope
This policy applies to arthroscopic knee surgery, with or without partial meniscectomy, for symptomatic meniscal tears in adult members.

## 2. Coverage Criteria
Knee arthroscopy is considered medically necessary when ALL of the following criteria are met:

2.1 Significant knee pain plus mechanical symptoms, such as locking, catching, or giving way.
2.2 Radiologic confirmation of the meniscal tear on MRI, based on the official written report.
2.3 At least 6 weeks of formal in-person physical therapy in the past year, confirmed by physical therapy notes or claims history.
2.4 No more than mild osteoarthritis, defined as Kellgren-Lawrence grade 0, 1, or 2, or modified Outerbridge grade 0, 1, or 2.

## 3. Exclusions and Exceptions
3.1 The physical therapy requirement is waived when the knee is locked by a displaced bucket-handle tear.
3.2 Home or virtual physical therapy does not meet criterion 2.3.

## 4. Documentation Requirements
4.1 Examination findings, the MRI report, the radiograph report with osteoarthritis grade, and physical therapy notes with dates.
