# Northwind Care Medical Policy NWC-SLP-401: Home Sleep Apnea Testing

Fictional policy for a synthetic-data demo. Adapted from Aetna Clinical Policy Bulletin 0004, Obstructive Sleep Apnea in Adults, section I.A.2 (https://www.aetna.com/cpb/medical/data/1_99/0004.html). The criteria are paraphrased and simplified; this is not the current coverage policy of any real payer.

## 1. Scope
Northwind Care covers unattended home sleep apnea testing (HSAT) for adults with suspected obstructive sleep apnea.

## 2. Coverage Criteria
Home sleep apnea test is considered medically necessary when ALL of the following criteria are met:

2.1 Symptoms suggestive of obstructive sleep apnea, such as loud snoring, witnessed apneas, or excessive daytime sleepiness.
2.2 The test is part of a comprehensive sleep evaluation with follow-up by the treating clinician.
2.3 The device is a Type II, Type III, or Type IV(A) monitor measuring airflow and at least 2 other channels, or a peripheral arterial tone device.

## 3. Exclusions and Exceptions
3.1 Members with a comorbid condition that calls for attended polysomnography (see the attended polysomnography policy).

## 4. Documentation Requirements
4.1 Symptom history, the sleep evaluation note, and the device type.
