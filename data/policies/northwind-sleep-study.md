# Northwind Care Medical Policy NWC-SLP-401: Home Sleep Apnea Testing

Fictional policy for a synthetic-data demo. Not a real payer's coverage policy.

## 1. Scope
Northwind Care covers home sleep apnea testing (HSAT) for adults with suspected obstructive sleep apnea.

## 2. Coverage Criteria
Home sleep apnea test is considered medically necessary when ALL of the following criteria are met:

2.1 Symptoms suggestive of obstructive sleep apnea, such as loud snoring, witnessed apneas, or excessive daytime sleepiness.
2.2 Epworth Sleepiness Scale score of 10 or more, or a STOP-BANG score of 3 or more.
2.3 No significant comorbidity that would make home testing inaccurate, such as severe COPD, heart failure, or neuromuscular disease.

## 3. Exclusions
3.1 Screening of asymptomatic members.

## 4. Documentation Requirements
4.1 Symptom history, questionnaire scores, and comorbidity review.
