# Northwind Care Medical Policy NWC-IMG-118: CT of the Abdomen and Pelvis for Suspected Pancreatitis

Fictional policy for a synthetic-data demo. Adapted from ACR Appropriateness Criteria, Acute Pancreatitis; ACR-NKF consensus statement on intravenous iodinated contrast in kidney disease (Radiology, 2020) (https://acsearch.acr.org/docs/69468/Narrative/). The criteria are paraphrased and simplified; this is not the current coverage policy of any real payer.

## 1. Scope
Northwind Care covers CT of the abdomen and pelvis with intravenous contrast for members with suspected or known acute pancreatitis.

## 2. Coverage Criteria
CT abdomen and pelvis is considered medically necessary when ALL of the following criteria are met:

2.1 Abdominal pain with a documented physical examination.
2.2 Diagnostic uncertainty after clinical, laboratory, and ultrasound evaluation; suspected complications such as necrosis more than 48 to 72 hours after symptom onset; or clinical deterioration.
2.3 Ultrasound was performed first, or was nondiagnostic or not appropriate for the clinical question.
2.4 For IV contrast, kidney function (eGFR) is documented; an eGFR below 30 mL/min/1.73m2 requires a documented risk-benefit decision by the ordering clinician and radiologist.

## 3. Exclusions and Exceptions
3.1 CT within the first 48 to 72 hours of a typical first episode when the diagnosis is already established by pain and lipase.

## 4. Documentation Requirements
4.1 Examination findings, laboratory results with dates, the ultrasound report, and the eGFR.
