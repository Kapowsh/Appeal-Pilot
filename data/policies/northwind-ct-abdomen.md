# Northwind Care Medical Policy NWC-IMG-118: CT of the Abdomen and Pelvis

Fictional policy for a synthetic-data demo. Not a real payer's coverage policy.

## 1. Scope
Northwind Care covers computed tomography of the abdomen and pelvis for evaluation of acute or persistent abdominal symptoms.

## 2. Coverage Criteria
CT abdomen and pelvis is considered medically necessary when ALL of the following criteria are met:

2.1 Acute or persistent abdominal pain with a documented physical examination.
2.2 Laboratory or prior imaging findings that suggest a specific pathology, such as elevated lipase, leukocytosis, or an abnormal ultrasound.
2.3 Ultrasound was performed first, or ultrasound is not appropriate for the suspected condition.
2.4 For contrast studies, renal function (eGFR of 30 mL/min/1.73m2 or higher) documented within 30 days.

## 3. Exclusions
3.1 Screening CT in asymptomatic members.

## 4. Documentation Requirements
4.1 Examination findings, laboratory results with dates, and any prior imaging reports.
