# Acme Health Medical Policy AH-RX-051: Adalimumab for Plaque Psoriasis

Fictional policy for a synthetic-data demo. Adapted from Aetna Clinical Policy Bulletin 0655, Adalimumab, plaque psoriasis (https://www.aetna.com/cpb/medical/data/600_699/0655.html). The criteria are paraphrased and simplified; this is not the current coverage policy of any real payer.

## 1. Scope
This policy applies to adalimumab for adults with moderate to severe plaque psoriasis who have not previously received a biologic.

## 2. Coverage Criteria
Biologic therapy for plaque psoriasis is considered medically necessary when ALL of the following criteria are met:

2.1 Crucial body areas are affected (hands, feet, face, neck, scalp, genitals or groin, or intertriginous areas), or at least 10% of body surface area (BSA) is affected.
2.2 If only 3% to 9% of BSA is affected and no crucial area is involved: inadequate response or intolerance to phototherapy or to methotrexate, cyclosporine, or acitretin, or a clinical reason to avoid those drugs.
2.3 Prescribed by or in consultation with a dermatologist.
2.4 A documented negative tuberculosis test within 12 months before starting therapy.

## 3. Exclusions and Exceptions
3.1 Concurrent use with another biologic or targeted synthetic drug.

## 4. Documentation Requirements
4.1 Affected body areas and BSA, prior therapies with dates, prescriber specialty, and the TB test result.
