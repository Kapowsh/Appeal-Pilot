# Acme Health Medical Policy AH-RX-051: Biologic Agents for Plaque Psoriasis

Fictional policy for a synthetic-data demo. Not a real payer's coverage policy.

## 1. Scope
This policy applies to biologic agents for adults with chronic plaque psoriasis.

## 2. Coverage Criteria
Biologic therapy for plaque psoriasis is considered medically necessary when ALL of the following criteria are met:

2.1 Moderate-to-severe plaque psoriasis, defined as body surface area (BSA) involvement of 10% or more, a PASI score of 12 or more, or involvement of the face, hands, feet, or genitals.
2.2 Inadequate response to at least 3 months of topical therapy, such as high-potency corticosteroids or vitamin D analogs.
2.3 Failure of, intolerance of, or contraindication to phototherapy or at least one conventional systemic agent (methotrexate, cyclosporine, or acitretin).
2.4 Negative tuberculosis screening within 12 months before starting therapy.
2.5 Prescribed by or in consultation with a dermatologist.

## 3. Exclusions
3.1 Concurrent use of two biologic agents.
3.2 Mild psoriasis controlled with topical therapy.

## 4. Documentation Requirements
4.1 BSA or PASI assessment, prior therapy names and dates, TB test result, and prescriber specialty.
