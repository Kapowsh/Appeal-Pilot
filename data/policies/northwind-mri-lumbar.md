# Northwind Care Medical Policy NWC-IMG-104: Advanced Imaging: Lumbar Spine MRI

Fictional policy for a synthetic-data demo. Not a real payer's coverage policy.

## 1. Scope
Northwind Care covers lumbar spine MRI for members with radicular pain or neurogenic claudication.

## 2. Coverage Criteria
MRI lumbar spine is considered medically necessary when ALL of the following criteria are met:

2.1 Radicular pain or neurogenic claudication for 4 weeks or more.
2.2 At least 4 weeks of conservative care, including supervised physical therapy.
2.3 Imaging is requested to plan an epidural injection or surgery, or a progressive neurologic deficit is documented.

## 3. Exclusions
3.1 Axial back pain without radicular features.

## 4. Documentation Requirements
4.1 Symptom duration, therapy records, and the intended use of the imaging result.
