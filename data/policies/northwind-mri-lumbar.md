# Northwind Care Medical Policy NWC-IMG-104: Advanced Imaging: Lumbar Spine MRI

Fictional policy for a synthetic-data demo. Adapted from Molina Clinical Policy, Lumbar Spine MRI (https://www.molinahealthcare.com/providers/tx/PDF/Marketplace/Policy/LumbarSpineMRI.pdf). The criteria are paraphrased and simplified; this is not the current coverage policy of any real payer.

## 1. Scope
Northwind Care covers lumbar spine MRI for members with low back pain and radiculopathy.

## 2. Coverage Criteria
MRI lumbar spine is considered medically necessary when ALL of the following criteria are met:

2.1 Low back pain with radiculopathy documented on examination.
2.2 Failure to respond to a 6-week trial of conservative care, including anti-inflammatory medication and activity modification.
2.3 The conservative care included at least one active treatment, such as physical therapy, chiropractic care, or a supervised home exercise program.

## 3. Exclusions and Exceptions
3.1 Progressive or new neurologic deficit, or symptoms of cauda equina syndrome, may be imaged without a trial of conservative care.

## 4. Documentation Requirements
4.1 Examination findings, the dates and types of conservative care, and the response to care.
