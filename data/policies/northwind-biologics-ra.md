# Northwind Care Medical Policy NWC-PH-210: Biologic DMARDs for Rheumatoid Arthritis

Fictional policy for a synthetic-data demo. Adapted from Aetna Clinical Policy Bulletin 0655, Adalimumab, rheumatoid arthritis (https://www.aetna.com/cpb/medical/data/600_699/0655.html). The criteria are paraphrased and simplified; this is not the current coverage policy of any real payer.

## 1. Scope
Northwind Care covers adalimumab for adults with moderately to severely active rheumatoid arthritis.

## 2. Coverage Criteria
Biologic therapy for rheumatoid arthritis is considered medically necessary when ALL of the following criteria are met:

2.1 Moderately to severely active rheumatoid arthritis.
2.2 A positive rheumatoid factor (RF) or anti-cyclic citrullinated peptide (anti-CCP) test, or testing of RF, anti-CCP, and CRP or ESR.
2.3 Failure to reach low disease activity after a trial of at least 3 months of methotrexate at a dose of 15 mg per week or more, or documented intolerance of or contraindication to methotrexate.
2.4 Prescribed by or in consultation with a rheumatologist.
2.5 A documented negative tuberculosis test within 12 months before starting therapy.

## 3. Exclusions and Exceptions
3.1 Combination with another biologic or targeted synthetic DMARD.

## 4. Documentation Requirements
4.1 Disease activity measure, RF and anti-CCP results, methotrexate dose and dates, TB result, and prescriber specialty.
