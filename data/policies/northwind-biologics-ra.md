# Northwind Care Medical Policy NWC-PH-210: Biologic DMARDs for Rheumatoid Arthritis

Fictional policy for a synthetic-data demo. Not a real payer's coverage policy.

## 1. Scope
Northwind Care covers biologic disease-modifying agents for adults with rheumatoid arthritis.

## 2. Coverage Criteria
Biologic therapy for rheumatoid arthritis is considered medically necessary when ALL of the following criteria are met:

2.1 Diagnosis of moderate-to-severe rheumatoid arthritis, such as a CDAI above 10 or a DAS28 above 3.2.
2.2 Inadequate response to at least 3 months of methotrexate at 15 mg per week or more, or documented intolerance of or contraindication to methotrexate.
2.3 Negative tuberculosis screening (IGRA or PPD) within 12 months.
2.4 Prescribed by a rheumatologist.

## 3. Exclusions
3.1 Combination with another biologic or targeted synthetic DMARD.

## 4. Documentation Requirements
4.1 Disease activity score, methotrexate dose and dates, TB result, and prescriber specialty.
