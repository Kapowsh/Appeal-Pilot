# Northwind Care Medical Policy NWC-ORTH-512: Total Hip Arthroplasty

Fictional policy for a synthetic-data demo. Not a real payer's coverage policy.

## 1. Scope
Northwind Care covers elective total hip arthroplasty for advanced hip arthritis.

## 2. Coverage Criteria
Total hip arthroplasty is considered medically necessary when ALL of the following criteria are met:

2.1 Advanced hip arthritis on radiographs, such as joint space narrowing and osteophytes consistent with Kellgren-Lawrence grade 3 or 4.
2.2 Persistent hip pain and functional limitation that interfere with activities of daily living.
2.3 At least 3 months of documented conservative therapy (physical therapy, NSAIDs, assistive device, or intra-articular injection) without adequate relief.
2.4 BMI below 40, or documented surgical clearance addressing perioperative risk.

## 3. Exclusions
3.1 Active joint or systemic infection.

## 4. Documentation Requirements
4.1 Radiograph report, functional assessment, conservative therapy names with start and end dates, and BMI.
