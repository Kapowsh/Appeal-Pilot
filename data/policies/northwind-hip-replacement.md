# Northwind Care Medical Policy NWC-ORTH-512: Total Hip Arthroplasty

Fictional policy for a synthetic-data demo. Adapted from Aetna Clinical Policy Bulletin 0287, Hip Arthroplasty (https://www.aetna.com/cpb/medical/data/200_299/0287.html). The criteria are paraphrased and simplified; this is not the current coverage policy of any real payer.

## 1. Scope
Northwind Care covers elective total hip arthroplasty for adults with osteoarthritis of the hip.

## 2. Coverage Criteria
Total hip arthroplasty is considered medically necessary when ALL of the following criteria are met:

2.1 Pain and functional disability from osteoarthritis that interfere with activities of daily living.
2.2 Examination findings of limited range of motion, antalgic gait, and pain in the hip joint with passive range of motion.
2.3 Moderate or severe osteoarthritis of the hip on imaging (Tonnis grade 2 or 3).
2.4 Unsuccessful conservative therapy for at least 12 weeks (at least 24 weeks with a relative contraindication such as BMI above 40 or age under 50), with at least half of it formal in-person physical therapy, documented in the medical record.

## 3. Exclusions and Exceptions
3.1 Conservative therapy is not required when it is not appropriate, such as progressive flexion contracture or avascular necrosis with collapse of the femoral head; the record must explain why.

## 4. Documentation Requirements
4.1 Imaging report with grade, functional assessment, conservative therapy types with start and end dates, BMI, and, for members with diabetes, an HbA1c below 8 percent within 3 months before surgery.
