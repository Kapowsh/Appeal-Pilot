# Acme Health Medical Policy AH-RAD-012: MRI of the Lumbar Spine

Fictional policy for a synthetic-data demo. Not a real payer's coverage policy.

## 1. Scope
This policy applies to magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) of the lumbar spine with or without contrast for adult members with low back pain.

## 2. Coverage Criteria
MRI lumbar spine is considered medically necessary when ALL of the following criteria are met:

2.1 Low back pain, with or without radicular symptoms, that has persisted for at least 6 weeks.
2.2 Failure of at least 6 weeks of physician-directed conservative therapy, including physical therapy, home exercise, or NSAIDs, documented in the medical record.
2.3 Objective neurologic findings on examination, such as a positive straight leg raise, dermatomal sensory loss, motor weakness, or reflex asymmetry.
2.4 The imaging result is expected to change management, such as planning an epidural steroid injection or surgical referral.

## 3. Exclusions
3.1 Uncomplicated acute low back pain of less than 6 weeks without red-flag signs.
3.2 Repeat imaging within 12 months without a new injury or change in neurologic status.

## 4. Documentation Requirements
4.1 Office notes documenting symptom onset and duration, conservative therapy dates, and the neurologic examination.
4.2 The ordering clinician's statement of how results will affect the treatment plan.
