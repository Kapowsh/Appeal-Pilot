# Acme Health Medical Policy AH-RAD-012: MRI of the Lumbar Spine

Fictional policy for a synthetic-data demo. Adapted from BlueCross BlueShield of South Carolina Medical Policy CAM 709, MRI Lumbar Spine (https://medicalpolicy.southcarolinablues.com/web/public/brands/medicalpolicy/external/external-policies/mri-lumbar-spine). The criteria are paraphrased and simplified; this is not the current coverage policy of any real payer.

## 1. Scope
This policy applies to MRI of the lumbar spine for adult members with low back pain and suspected lumbar radiculopathy.

## 2. Coverage Criteria
MRI lumbar spine is considered medically necessary when ALL of the following criteria are met:

2.1 Low back pain with radicular symptoms or signs, such as pain radiating into the leg, dermatomal sensory change, or a positive straight leg raise.
2.2 Conservative treatment for at least 6 weeks within the last 6 months, including at least one active component (such as physical therapy or a supervised home exercise program) and at least one inactive component (such as anti-inflammatory medication or activity modification).
2.3 Failure of conservative treatment, defined as lack of meaningful improvement after a full course of treatment, progression or worsening of symptoms during treatment, or a documented medical reason the member cannot participate.

## 3. Exclusions and Exceptions
3.1 Conservative treatment is not required when a new neurological deficit that localizes to the lumbar spine is documented on examination, such as extremity muscle weakness or new foot drop.
3.2 Inconvenience or unexplained noncompliance does not count as inability to complete conservative treatment.

## 4. Documentation Requirements
4.1 Office notes with symptom onset and duration, the dates and components of conservative treatment, the response to treatment, and the neurologic examination.
