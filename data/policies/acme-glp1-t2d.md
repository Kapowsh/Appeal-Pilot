# Acme Health Medical Policy AH-RX-044: GLP-1 Receptor Agonists for Type 2 Diabetes

Fictional policy for a synthetic-data demo. Adapted from OptumRx criteria for Indiana Medicaid, GLP-1 Receptor Agonists and Combinations (07.01.2026), preferred agents (Ozempic) (https://contenthub-aem.optumrx.com/content/dam/contenthub/retail-pharmacy-override/assets/customsite-indiana/documents/quicklinks/20260701_Public-Facing_GLP-1%20RA-GIP-Combinations_PA_Final.pdf). The criteria are paraphrased and simplified; this is not the current coverage policy of any real payer.

## 1. Scope
This policy applies to preferred GLP-1 receptor agonists prescribed for adults with type 2 diabetes mellitus.

## 2. Coverage Criteria
GLP-1 receptor agonist for type 2 diabetes is considered medically necessary when ALL of the following criteria are met:

2.1 The member is 18 years of age or older.
2.2 Diagnosis of type 2 diabetes mellitus, confirmed by chart documentation or claims history.
2.3 A previous trial of metformin for at least 90 days within the past 120 days, or documented intolerance or contraindication to metformin.
2.4 A baseline HbA1c obtained within the past 90 days before starting the GLP-1 receptor agonist.

## 3. Exclusions and Exceptions
3.1 Use for weight loss alone, without type 2 diabetes.
3.2 Concurrent use with a DPP-4 inhibitor or another GLP-1 receptor agonist.

## 4. Documentation Requirements
4.1 HbA1c result with date, metformin dose history with dates, and the current medication list. Initial approval is for up to 6 months.
