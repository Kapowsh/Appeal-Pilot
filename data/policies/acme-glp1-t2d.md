# Acme Health Medical Policy AH-RX-044: GLP-1 Receptor Agonists for Type 2 Diabetes

Fictional policy for a synthetic-data demo. Not a real payer's coverage policy.

## 1. Scope
This policy applies to GLP-1 receptor agonists prescribed for glycemic control in adults with type 2 diabetes mellitus.

## 2. Coverage Criteria
GLP-1 receptor agonist for type 2 diabetes is considered medically necessary when ALL of the following criteria are met:

2.1 Diagnosis of type 2 diabetes mellitus (ICD-10 E11.x).
2.2 HbA1c of 7.0% or higher measured within the last 90 days.
2.3 Inadequate response to, intolerance of, or contraindication to metformin after at least 90 days at a maximally tolerated dose.
2.4 The drug is not prescribed for weight loss alone and is not combined with another GLP-1 receptor agonist or a DPP-4 inhibitor.

## 3. Exclusions
3.1 Use for weight management without a type 2 diabetes diagnosis.
3.2 Type 1 diabetes mellitus.

## 4. Documentation Requirements
4.1 Recent HbA1c result with date, metformin dose history and dates, and the current medication list.
