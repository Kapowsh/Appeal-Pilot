# Northwind Care Medical Policy NWC-RX-220: Non-Preferred Incretin Therapy for Type 2 Diabetes

Fictional policy for a synthetic-data demo. Adapted from OptumRx criteria for Indiana Medicaid, GLP-1 Receptor Agonists and Combinations (07.01.2026), non-preferred agents (Mounjaro) (https://contenthub-aem.optumrx.com/content/dam/contenthub/retail-pharmacy-override/assets/customsite-indiana/documents/quicklinks/20260701_Public-Facing_GLP-1%20RA-GIP-Combinations_PA_Final.pdf). The criteria are paraphrased and simplified; this is not the current coverage policy of any real payer.

## 1. Scope
Northwind Care covers non-preferred incretin agents, such as tirzepatide, for members with type 2 diabetes.

## 2. Coverage Criteria
GLP-1 receptor agonist for type 2 diabetes is considered medically necessary when ALL of the following criteria are met:

2.1 Diagnosis of type 2 diabetes mellitus, confirmed by chart documentation or claims history.
2.2 A baseline HbA1c obtained within the past 90 days before starting the requested agent.
2.3 The member is not taking a DPP-4 inhibitor, or is transitioning off one within a 45-day transition period, and will not use another GLP-1 receptor agonist at the same time.
2.4 Previous trial and failure of two different preferred GLP-1 receptor agonists, or a documented medical reason they cannot be used.

## 3. Exclusions and Exceptions
3.1 Use for weight loss without type 2 diabetes.

## 4. Documentation Requirements
4.1 HbA1c with date, names and dates of prior GLP-1 receptor agonists, and the reason for failure.
