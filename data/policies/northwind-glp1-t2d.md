# Northwind Care Medical Policy NWC-RX-220: Incretin Therapy for Type 2 Diabetes

Fictional policy for a synthetic-data demo. Not a real payer's coverage policy.

## 1. Scope
Northwind Care covers GLP-1 and dual GIP/GLP-1 receptor agonists for adults with type 2 diabetes.

## 2. Coverage Criteria
GLP-1 receptor agonist for type 2 diabetes is considered medically necessary when ALL of the following criteria are met:

2.1 Diagnosis of type 2 diabetes mellitus.
2.2 HbA1c of 7.5% or higher within the last 6 months.
2.3 A trial of metformin and one additional oral agent (sulfonylurea, SGLT2 inhibitor, or thiazolidinedione) for at least 3 months each, or a contraindication to them.

## 3. Exclusions
3.1 Use for weight loss without type 2 diabetes.

## 4. Documentation Requirements
4.1 HbA1c with date and the names, doses, and dates of prior agents.
