# Acme Health Medical Policy AH-SLP-007: Attended (In-Lab) Polysomnography

Fictional policy for a synthetic-data demo. Not a real payer's coverage policy.

## 1. Scope
This policy applies to attended in-laboratory polysomnography for suspected obstructive sleep apnea in adults. Home sleep apnea testing (HSAT) is the preferred first test.

## 2. Coverage Criteria
Attended polysomnography is considered medically necessary when ALL of the following criteria are met:

2.1 Clinical suspicion of obstructive sleep apnea, with a STOP-BANG score of 3 or more or an Epworth Sleepiness Scale score greater than 10.
2.2 Home sleep apnea testing is inappropriate or nondiagnostic, for example due to significant cardiopulmonary disease, neuromuscular disease, suspected central sleep apnea, or a prior negative or technically inadequate HSAT with persistent symptoms.
2.3 Face-to-face evaluation by the treating clinician within the last 6 months documenting sleep-related symptoms.

## 3. Exclusions
3.1 In-lab testing as a first test in uncomplicated adults who can complete a home test.

## 4. Documentation Requirements
4.1 Sleep questionnaire scores, comorbidity documentation, and any prior HSAT report.
