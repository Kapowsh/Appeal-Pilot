# Acme Health Medical Policy AH-SLP-007: Attended (In-Lab) Polysomnography

Fictional policy for a synthetic-data demo. Adapted from Aetna Clinical Policy Bulletin 0004, Obstructive Sleep Apnea in Adults, section I.A.1 (https://www.aetna.com/cpb/medical/data/1_99/0004.html). The criteria are paraphrased and simplified; this is not the current coverage policy of any real payer.

## 1. Scope
This policy applies to attended in-facility polysomnography to diagnose obstructive sleep apnea in adults. Home sleep apnea testing is the expected first test for other members.

## 2. Coverage Criteria
Attended polysomnography is considered medically necessary when ALL of the following criteria are met:

2.1 Symptoms suggestive of obstructive sleep apnea, such as loud snoring, witnessed apneas, or excessive daytime sleepiness.
2.2 The test is part of a comprehensive sleep evaluation with follow-up by the treating clinician.
2.3 At least one indication for an attended study: a comorbid condition (moderate to severe pulmonary disease such as COPD, neuromuscular disease, stroke with residual respiratory effects, epilepsy, congestive heart failure with NYHA class III or IV or LVEF below 45%, pulmonary hypertension, chronic opioid use, BMI above 45, or obesity hypoventilation syndrome); a comorbid sleep disorder such as central sleep apnea; a negative or technically inadequate home test; low pretest probability; or inability to use home equipment safely.

## 3. Exclusions and Exceptions
3.1 Attended testing as a first test in uncomplicated adults who can complete a home test.

## 4. Documentation Requirements
4.1 Symptom history, the comorbid condition or other indication, and any prior home test report.
