# Acme Health Medical Policy AH-SURG-022: Bariatric Surgery for Co-Morbid Conditions Related to Obesity

Fictional policy for a synthetic-data demo. Adapted from CMS National Coverage Determination 100.1, Bariatric Surgery for Treatment of Co-Morbid Conditions Related to Morbid Obesity (https://www.cms.gov/medicare-coverage-database/view/ncd.aspx?ncdid=57). The criteria are paraphrased and simplified; this is not the current coverage policy of any real payer.

## 1. Scope
This policy applies to open and laparoscopic Roux-en-Y gastric bypass, laparoscopic adjustable gastric banding, biliopancreatic diversion with duodenal switch, and sleeve gastrectomy.

## 2. Coverage Criteria
Bariatric surgery is considered medically necessary when ALL of the following criteria are met:

2.1 Body mass index (BMI) of 35 or higher.
2.2 At least one co-morbidity related to obesity. Type 2 diabetes mellitus counts as a co-morbidity.
2.3 The member has previously been unsuccessful with medical treatment for obesity.

## 3. Exclusions and Exceptions
3.1 BMI below 35, regardless of co-morbidities.

## 4. Documentation Requirements
4.1 Measured height, weight, and BMI with date; co-morbidity diagnoses; and records of prior medical weight-loss treatment and its outcome.
