# Acme Health Medical Policy AH-SURG-022: Bariatric Surgery for Severe Obesity

Fictional policy for a synthetic-data demo. Not a real payer's coverage policy.

## 1. Scope
This policy applies to laparoscopic Roux-en-Y gastric bypass and sleeve gastrectomy for adults with severe obesity.

## 2. Coverage Criteria
Bariatric surgery is considered medically necessary when ALL of the following criteria are met:

2.1 The member is 18 years of age or older.
2.2 Body mass index (BMI) of 40 or higher, or BMI of 35 or higher with at least one obesity-related comorbidity (type 2 diabetes, hypertension, or obstructive sleep apnea).
2.3 Participation in a medically supervised weight-management program for at least 6 months within the last 2 years.
2.4 Psychological evaluation clearing the member for surgery.

## 3. Exclusions
3.1 BMI below 35 regardless of comorbidities.
3.2 Revision procedures, which are covered under a separate policy.

## 4. Documentation Requirements
4.1 Measured height, weight, and BMI with date, comorbidity diagnoses, weight-program records, and the psychological evaluation.
